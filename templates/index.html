User Guide & Technical Architecture

About VidSeek AI

VidSeek AI is an intelligent lecture comprehension engine that transforms long unstructured video lectures into segmented topic-wise mini-videos, synchronized transcripts, timestamped semantic search, and interactive AI study tools.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

How to Use VidSeek AI

Master any complex video lecture in 6 simple steps

1

Provide Any Video

Paste a YouTube URL or direct video link in the URL bar, drag-and-drop a local video file (MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV) from your computer, or click "Try Sample Lecture" for an instant demonstration.

2

Automated AI Pipeline

The system extracts the high-fidelity 16kHz audio stream, generates timed speech transcripts using OpenAI Whisper, and applies NLP topic boundary detection to pinpoint natural conceptual shifts.

3

FFmpeg Video Slicing

Using fast stream-copy slicing (no re-encoding delay or quality degradation), the long video is separated into individual topic-wise mini video clips with dedicated thumbnails and start/end time markers.

4

Search & Jump Timestamps

Type any keyword or concept into the search box to get instant ranked results. Click "Jump to Timestamp" to seek the master player to the exact second that concept is discussed.

5

Ask AI & Download Notes

Ask natural questions about the lecture to receive AI explanations accompanied by direct video citations. Download comprehensive lecture summary notes in Markdown format for offline study.

6

Persistent Video History

Every video you analyze is securely saved to your local History library. Switch between different videos at any time, review previous transcripts, or resume where you left off with a single click.

ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE

VidSeek AI Pipeline Architecture

How raw audiovisual streams turn into structured, queryable knowledge

Phase 1 • Audio Ingestion

FFmpeg Audio Extraction

Extracts uncompressed 16,000 Hz mono PCM WAV audio stream directly from MP4/MKV container. Audio channels are normalized for optimal speech clarity and acoustic feature isolation.

FFmpeg 16kHz Mono PCM yt-dlp Stream
Phase 2 • Speech-to-Text

OpenAI Whisper AI

Processes acoustic audio frames through deep transformer encoder-decoder blocks to produce high-accuracy speech transcription with millisecond word timestamps and phrase confidence scoring.

Whisper Transformer Sub-second Timestamps Multilingual
Phase 3 • NLP Topic Detection

Boundary & Shift Discovery

Computes lexical cohesion and semantic transition dips across sliding sentence windows. Identifies natural topic boundaries and generates descriptive chapter headings automatically.

TextTiling Dips TF-IDF Vectorization Auto-Heading Naming
Phase 4 • Media Slicing & Search

Lossless Slicing & Query Index

Slices video clips using keyframe stream-copy to generate standalone mini-videos in seconds. Builds an inverted index for sub-millisecond keyword lookup and semantic Q&A matching.

Stream Copy Cutting Timestamp Search Semantic Q&A Engine

Pro Tips for the Best Experience

  • YouTube Videos: You can paste any standard YouTube link (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...) or shortened URL (https://youtu.be/...).
  • Interactive Transcript: Click on any segment in the synchronized transcript pane to instantly jump the video playback to that exact word.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Use Spacebar to pause/play, Left/Right arrow keys to jump 5 seconds, and M to mute.
  • Topic Mini-Videos: Each chapter card has its own dedicated player and download link so you can share or archive specific lecture sections.
